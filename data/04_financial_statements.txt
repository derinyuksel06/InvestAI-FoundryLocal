Four important company financial statements are the balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement, and statement of shareholders' equity.

A balance sheet shows what a company owns and what it owes at a particular point in time.

An income statement shows a company's revenue, expenses, and financial performance over a period of time.

A cash flow statement shows how cash moves into and out of a company.

The statement of shareholders' equity shows changes in the ownership value attributable to shareholders.

Financial statements can help investors understand a company's financial condition and performance, but they should be considered together rather than relying on only one figure.
