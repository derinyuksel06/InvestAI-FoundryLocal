Understanding a 10-K: Risk Factors and MD&A

Source: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

A Form 10-K is an annual report filed by many U.S. public companies with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

Item 1A, Risk Factors, contains information about significant risks that apply to the company or its securities.

Risk factors may relate to the company itself, its industry, geographic areas, or broader economic conditions.

The Risk Factors section primarily describes risks rather than explaining how management plans to solve them.

Management's Discussion and Analysis, commonly called MD&A, provides management's perspective on the company's financial condition and results of operations.

MD&A includes discussion of areas such as liquidity, capital resources, and results of operations.

Investors can use MD&A together with the financial statements and Risk Factors section to better understand the company's performance and major uncertainties.
