SOURCE: Investor.gov - Asset Allocation and Diversification

Diversification means spreading money across different investments rather than depending on a single investment.

The goal of diversification is to reduce the effect that poor performance from one investment may have on the overall portfolio.

A portfolio can be diversified across different asset categories, such as stocks and bonds, and also within each asset category.

Mutual funds and ETFs can make diversification easier because one fund may hold many different investments.

Diversification can help manage risk, but it cannot guarantee against investment losses.
