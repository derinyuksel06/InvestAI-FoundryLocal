Investment Fund Fees and Expenses

Source: Investor.gov — U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

Mutual funds and ETFs have operating costs.

Funds can pass these costs to investors through fees and expenses.

Fees and expenses reduce investment returns.

Even relatively small differences in fees can lead to meaningful differences in investment returns over long periods of time.

Annual fund operating expenses are generally deducted from the fund's assets.

One common measure of fund costs is the expense ratio.

Investors should consider fees and expenses when comparing mutual funds or ETFs.

A higher-cost fund must perform better than a lower-cost fund in order to produce the same return for an investor.
