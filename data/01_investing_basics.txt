SOURCE: Investor.gov - Introduction to Investing

Investing means putting money into assets such as stocks or bonds with the expectation of earning a return over time.

Investment returns can come from an increase in an asset's value, interest payments, or dividends.

All investments involve risk. The value of investments can rise or fall over time.

Before choosing investments, investors should consider their goals, time horizon, and tolerance for risk.

Stocks represent ownership in a company. Bonds generally represent money lent to a company or government.

Asset allocation and diversification are two important ways to manage investment risk.
