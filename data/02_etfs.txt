SOURCE: Investor.gov - Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs)

An exchange-traded fund, or ETF, is an investment fund whose shares can be bought and sold on an exchange.

ETFs can hold stocks, bonds, or other assets.

Many ETFs hold investments across multiple companies or industries. This can help investors diversify instead of relying on one individual investment.

However, not every ETF is highly diversified. Some ETFs may hold only a small number of investments or may focus on a very specific asset or sector.

An investor who buys an ETF owns a proportional interest in the fund's portfolio.
